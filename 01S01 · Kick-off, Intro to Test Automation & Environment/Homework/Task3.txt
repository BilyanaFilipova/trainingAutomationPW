Verify Priority Selection and Its Impact on the Request
Manual testing – Not recommended for automation
Objective: To verify whether changing the Priority level has any impact on the submitted service request.

Steps:
1. Open the New Request Wizard.
2. Enter all required information in Step 1.
3. Select Low priority and continue completing the wizard.
4. Repeat the process using Medium, High and Urgent priorities.
5. Submit each request.
6. Check the Review & Submit page and the submitted request details.
7. Compare the results for all four priority levels.
Test data:
- Priority: Low
- Priority: Medium
- Priority: High
- Priority: Urgent
Expected result:
- The selected Priority is displayed correctly during request creation.
- The selected value should be reflected in the submitted request details.
- Any priority-related behavior should be consistent with the selected value.
Why should this NOT be automated?
- Lack of Stability: The Priority field currently does not appear to have any impact on the submitted request or other parts of the application.
- The expected business behavior is unclear and may change following future business decisions.
- Automating this scenario now could lead to maintaining test scripts for functionality that is not yet properly defined.
